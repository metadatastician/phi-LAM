= ADR 0007: Establish the pre-runtime proof frontier
:status: accepted
:date: 2026-08-01

== Context

The semantic seed had proved idempotence and model-parametric preservation, and
the pure reference scheduler had discharged the required at-most-once law. Two
questions remained before operational engineering: whether the rewrite system
had a complete strategy-independent metatheory, and whether scheduler states
carried enough structure to exclude incoherent histories.

== Decision

Strengthen the independent Lean development to prove:

* logical and executable normality are equivalent;
* every primitive rewrite strictly decreases sequence length;
* no infinite rewrite chain exists;
* derivation length exactly accounts for removed actions;
* every rewrite and derivation leaves `normalize` invariant;
* all derivations from one input are joinable at its normalized result;
* every reachable normal form equals that result; and
* reachable normal forms are unique.

Promote trace/removal accounting from an Idris bounded test to the general
theorems `rewriteRemovesOne`, `derivationLengthAccounting`, and
`normalizationLengthAccounting`.

Define reference-scheduler coherence with two requirements: the seen set has no
duplicate identity, and reversing its newest-first representation produces
exactly the at-most-once identities in effective acceptance order. Prove the
empty state coherent and prove every scheduling transition, action,
interpretation, and instrumented execution preserves coherence.

Finally, classify each at-most-once attempt with dependent evidence. An
accepted attempt carries proof that the identity was unseen, the exact accepted
state transition, and an empty diagnostic. A suppressed attempt carries proof
that the identity was seen, effective state is unchanged, and exactly one
suppression diagnostic is emitted.

== Consequences

For the current single rewrite rule and pure scheduler, the remaining central
properties are no longer bounded-test claims. The executable suite retains the
781-sequence corpus as regression and cross-language correspondence evidence.

Further useful proofs now require a new specification or implementation
subject. Crash recovery needs a durable acceptance/commit protocol; concurrency
needs an atomicity and linearization boundary; external effects need an adapter
contract; sandbox isolation needs a threat model and Zig implementation; and
full Idris–Lean equivalence needs a verified translation or shared extracted
core. None of those properties is implied by the pure reference model.
