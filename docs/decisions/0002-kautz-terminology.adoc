= ADR 0002: Quarantine the “Kautz Type 6” label
:status: accepted
:date: 2026-08-01

== Context

The discarded scaffold used the phrase “Kautz Type 6” without a mathematical
definition, source, representation, invariant, or executable construction.
That phrase therefore cannot presently identify an implemented property.

== Decision

phi-LAM makes no Kautz-graph claim. The phrase may re-enter project vocabulary
only through a later decision record that supplies, at minimum:

* a precise definition and notation;
* the relationship to established Kautz graph terminology;
* the role of the structure in phi-LAM semantics;
* machine-checkable invariants and counterexamples; and
* tests or proofs tied to the implementation.

Until then, “Kautz Type 6” is an unresolved research note, not a component,
feature, or architectural fact.
